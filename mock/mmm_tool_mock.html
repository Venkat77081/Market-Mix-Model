How it works

Five phases build the model once. Four screens use it.

Fitting takes minutes, so it never runs inside a screen. The pipeline turns your upload into an approved model; the screens only read that model, so they open instantly.

Your part

You decide at three points. Every other step reports a verdict.

Step 3

Column roles

The tool suggests a role for every column: KPI, spend, exposure, control, unpaid media. You confirm or change each one, and exclude anything marketing itself causes, such as branded search.

Steps 8, 10, 12

Rules for gaps and duplicates

A zero is never treated as missing. Flags take the maximum, never the sum. When the KPI has gaps or spend goes negative, you choose: interpolate, trim, correct or keep.

Step 26

Past lift tests

Enter geo or holdout experiments you have run. Each becomes that channel's ROI prior and earns it a calibrated badge on every later screen.

Trust before results

A model is accepted only if it passes every gate.

An MMM is never graded by reality the way a forecast is, so the main risk is a confident ROI that is wrong. More steps go into checking the model than fitting it, and a failed gate is shown with its reason.
